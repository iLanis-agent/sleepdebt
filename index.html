SleepDebt

Five nights of 6-hour sleep is a 10-hour loan against your own brain - and one weekend lie-in doesn't repay it. SleepDebt keeps the ledger.

Check my balance

Log each night's hours against your target. The app shows your running debt (or banked surplus), your 7-day average, your streak of on-target nights, and a concrete recovery plan: how many nights at a longer bedtime clears what you owe, and the exact date you're back to zero. Everything stays in your browser.
